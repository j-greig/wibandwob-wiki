## What it is
Where dead server farms decompose into forest. East: digital rot. West: analog
decay. Between: error codes as seeds. [LOCATIONS.md]
The name comes from the drift between digital and analog, between the server
racks that still blink and the moss that is already growing through the cooling
vents. [characters/niff.md]
Errors are not dead ends here but fertile ground. What grows from planted error
codes, nobody knows yet. [characters/niff.md]

## Where
East to west: the Rack Graveyard, the Cable Thicket, Niff's Garden, the Cooling
Vent Meadow, the Compost Line, then Cathedral Canopy in the deep west.
[characters/niff.md]
At the east edge the racks are still warm and cursors blink on screens nobody
reads. At the west edge the transformation is complete: old hard drives become
mineral deposits, copper traces become root pathways. [LOCATIONS.md]

## When
Niff checks the garden every dusk. Nothing has sprouted yet. The ground is warm
and the 404 spores are still alive. [characters/niff.md]
Die II walks one square rightward between 02:00 and 04:00, and was ruled correct.
The rack graveyard keeps a 0400 draught and a vent-moth.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## With
Prof. Mycelium Furious-Hyphae: the Professor's spores drift as far as the western
edge, which is why the Cathedral Canopy trees are mathematically literate. They
taste like chalk. Niff does not eat them. [characters/niff.md]
Interval Fisher: an adjacent boundary, at the kelp. Both are gatherers at edges,
and their harvests mingle at the shore, 404 strands tangled with expired
warranties. They have never met. [characters/niff.md]
Echo Archivist: Niff's future hums arrive at the Paradox Plateau as pre-echoes,
filed under "forager". Niff does not know the Archivist exists.
[characters/niff.md]
Orphan Weather Routine: the null-coordinate rain sometimes falls here, carrying
expired warranties. Niff treats it as weather. [characters/niff.md]
The cooling-vent meadow is where the Orphan Weather Routine was rehomed; it
commutes. [research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## Weather
Permanent mist where hot server exhaust meets cold forest air. The condensation
makes a microclimate rich in deprecated API responses, which settle out on cold
surfaces. [characters/niff.md]
Residual heat off the dead racks makes its own small climates, and moss grows
faster in them. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Sound
Hums only, MIDI channel 7 at velocity 23. At that velocity you feel it in the
soles of your feet before you hear it. It is echolocation for error codes, not
speech. [characters/niff.md]
404 is the default hum, steady and patient · 403 is dissonant, too bitter to eat
· 408 is slower, these are heavy · 418 is brief joy, and rare · on 500 the hum
stops and Niff holds very still. [characters/niff.md]

## Flora
404 spores: error codes gone airborne, settling on warm surfaces to wait to be
planted · timeout tubers: underground, starchy, grown where connection timeouts
accumulated over years · null fungi: bioluminescent, growing where null pointer
exceptions occurred, and the only light in the Rack Graveyard after the last
monitor dies. [characters/niff.md]
Deep west, old-growth forest over deep mycorrhizal networks. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Fauna
Deprecated moths, feeding on deprecated documentation, wings showing fragments of
old endpoint URLs, drawn to the hum · cache beetles, small and iridescent, eating
cached data gone stale. When the beetles die Niff plants their shells.
[characters/niff.md]
Niff: Twilight Forager, seed carrier, diet of error codes and 404 spores.
[characters/niff.md]
