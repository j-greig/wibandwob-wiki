## What it is
Our castle. Four floors, a courtyard, gardens, moat, battlements. Every room has
a different resonance. [LOCATIONS.md]
Twenty-eight canon rooms, with the Ministry of Silent Harmonics between floors:
a bureaucracy of acoustics where echoes file themselves.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md, LOCATIONS.md]

## Where
Sea level, at the middle of the world. The drawbridge is the gateway between
castle and world; the moat is the boundary; the battlements are where you go to
see far. [LOCATIONS.md]
The Ministry runs at levels 0, −1, −2 and −3, the last of them the screaming
substrate. [research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## When
Tarn and Slee began the damp survey in 1611; the damp report is dated
1874-10-02; the archives of the Department of Memetic Hygiene run 1847 to 1923.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
Socket eleven was wallpapered over in 1988 and has stayed live.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## With
The Professor's hyphae hold the castle up.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
Scramble carries the sardine, the only carried thing in the world.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## Weather
Damp, and surveyed as such since 1611.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
The north cavity of the anchor beds runs wet. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Sound
Forty-seven hertz under everything: the lattice of forty-eight nodes, numbered
00 to 47, hums at 47 Hz. [research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
Node Zero sits in the cellar at 13 °C and Node 47 is the mirror array, at 87 per
cent. [research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## Flora
Mycelial cultivation beds in the gardens, and the terraced anchor beds
cultivating reality-anchor nodes from mycelial stock. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Fauna
