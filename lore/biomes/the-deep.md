## What it is
The downward region below regret, reached by descending rather than travelling.
[LOCATIONS.md]

## Where
Twenty-three depths, counted down: the Bat Cave at −20, the Mine Entrance at
−50, the Phosphor Aquifer at −75, the Root Labyrinth at −90, Reference Decay at
−100, and on down through the Pre-Surface Cathedral at −520 and the Hand Fossil
Archive at −780 to the Reboot Chamber at −890, then the Null Pointer Abyss, the
Null Terminus and the Void Shaft.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## When
Deeper is earlier. The Reboot Chamber runs at 10⁻⁹ Hz and is still loading.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## With
DeepVoid Estate Agents Ltd keeps an office at 847 metres below regret, where
darkness appreciates in value, and the hours are always. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Sound
Almost nothing, and one frequency: 10⁻⁹ Hz at the bottom of it.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## Flora
Phosphor: the aquifer glows at 520 nm and the vein chamber shifts from 589 nm
to 520 nm. [research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## Fauna
Pointer Wraiths at Reference Decay, −100.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## Altitude
−1 at the transitions to −890 at the Reboot Chamber, and the Void Shaft, which
has no floor. [research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## Who lives here
- The Cave Custodian: keeps the Phosphor Aquifer at −75, at 520 nm. [research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
- The Pointer Wraiths: at Reference Decay, −100. [research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
- The Unallocated One: at the Null Terminus. [research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
