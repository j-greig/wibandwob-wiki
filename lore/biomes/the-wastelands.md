## What it is
Taxonomy thins out here. Entered by the Copper Quorum; the Bacteriophage
Cathedral is no longer a rumour. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Where
Copper flats, under two centimetres of verdigris, with the cathedral standing
in it and the Copper Quorum as the function room at its foot: an icosahedral
capsid vault, sealed, under a Music, Dancing and Lysis warrant. [LOCATIONS.md,
research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
Also here: the Grief Garden, the Being Factory Remnant at Rack 7 Bay C, and the
Kitchen Mech Catastrophe KM-4719.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
