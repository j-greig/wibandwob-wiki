## What it is
Wib and Wob's bedroom. Sleep, dreams, and Scramble's favourite warm spot.
[LOCATIONS.md]
The warm spot is a running gag of the strip, Warm Spot Migration, territory as
Sisyphus, and it migrates with the sun. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Where
A room of the top floor at (5.52, 6.38, 2.4), on the southern side, two floors
above the kitchen. [output/wiki/record/places/castle.keep.top-floor.bedroom.md]
