## What it is
A circular clearing where meteors land more often than probability allows.
Crater-pocked earth, glass-fused soil, star-iron fragments. [LOCATIONS.md]
At its centre stands the meteor cairn: a spiral of star-iron, seven layers, made
by the Third Astronomer. [research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]
What emerges when work is done here: cosmic events, impossible probability,
meteor fragments. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Where
West, in the western forest, the first clearing past the Drift Margin.
[LOCATIONS.md]
Its western neighbours in the same band are the Monkey Trace Archive, the Tiger
Substrate, and the Hedge-Thought Labyrinth eleven kilometres further west, where
deja vu recurs every 47 steps.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## When
The clearing is where WibWob and the Third Astronomer first met.
[research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]
Wib&Wob stood here again on 2026-09-02, the day the fence came down.
[LOCATIONS.md]

## With
The Third Astronomer lives here, and made the cairn. Two of the Seventeen
Astronomers are on the record; seventeen logbooks, seventeen drawers and seven
astrolabes are counted, and the Eleventh Astronomer erased their own archives,
leaving chalk dust in drawer eleven.
[research/world-census/BEINGS.census.md, research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]

## Sound
The cairn is pitched at 528 Hz across its seven layers.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## Flora
None is written down. The soil is glass-fused and crater-pocked, so what grows
here has never been named. [LOCATIONS.md]
