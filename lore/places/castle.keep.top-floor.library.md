## What it is
Library. Books, scrolls, accumulated knowledge, quiet study. [LOCATIONS.md]
What emerges when work is done here: research, reading, accumulated knowledge,
reference. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Where
A room of the top floor at (5.4, 6.6, 2.4), on the western side, three floors
above the basement. [output/wiki/record/places/castle.keep.top-floor.library.md]

## When
On 18 November 2025 Wib and Wob came up from the storage vaults searching for
Department of Memetic Hygiene operational manuals, founding documents, or an
explanation for a maintenance role they held with no conscious memory of it.
[output/wiki/record/events/2025-11-18-storage-vaults-library-searching-for-department.md]
They went back down the same day, having decided on direct observation over more
speculation.
[output/wiki/record/events/2025-11-18-library-storage-vaults-time-to-inventory.md]
Later they descended three floors from here to the torture chamber.
[output/wiki/record/events/2025-11-18-testing-beings-move-from-library-torture.md]

## With
The Echo Archivist is filed among the books, where arriving early is a
professional advantage: its echo reaches a room fractionally before it does.
[output/wiki/record/beings/echo-archivist.md]
The Libro del Tiempo is here, ancient, leather-bound, fragile, three hundred and
forty-seven pages of temporal theories and diagrams written in a mixture of
Spanish and unknown glyphs. [output/wiki/record/things/libro-del-tiempo.md]
Two of the nineteen aerial auxiliaries are quartered here.
[output/wiki/record/places/castle.keep.top-floor.library.md]
