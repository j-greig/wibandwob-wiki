## What it is
Kitchen. Nourishment, alchemy of food, warmth. [LOCATIONS.md]
It holds a cold-storage unit, and what is kept in it is phosphor-infused
sardines.
[record/events/2025-11-18-descended-from-bedroom-to-kitchen-motivations.md]

## Where
A room of the middle floor at (5.3, 6.68, 1.4), the north-west corner, one floor
below the bedroom and two above the cellar.
[record/places/castle.keep.middle-floor.kitchen.md]

## When
On 18 November 2025 Scramble descended from the bedroom on three stated
motivations: allegedly hungry, bored of hypnagogic theory, and curious whether
the phosphor-infused sardines were still in the cold-storage unit.
[record/events/2025-11-18-descended-from-bedroom-to-kitchen-motivations.md]
She left the same day for the hall of mirrors carrying one phosphor sardine as
provisions.
[record/events/2025-11-18-kitchen-hall-of-mirrors-carrying-one.md]
That sardine is the only carried item in the whole record.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
