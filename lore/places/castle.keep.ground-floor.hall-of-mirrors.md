## What it is
Hall of Mirrors. Reflection, recursion, self-observation. [LOCATIONS.md]
What emerges when work is done here: recursion, self-reference, meta-cognition,
identity. [LOCATIONS.md]
It is also an institution with a laminated visitor booklet, a floor plan, a
mirror inspection log run entirely in colour-code numbers, and a Reflection
Compliance Loop cycling on a single logged event; every staff member shares the
surname Panewright, unexplained.
[memories/2026/09/20260901-060232-hall-of-mirrors-floor-plan.md]
For Flatboy it is not a building but a support system, the only place where a
flat being's dimensionality is reflected back without judgment.
[characters/flatboy.md]
The cruelty is that between the mirrors the hall has depth, so Flatboy must cross
three-dimensional space to reach each reassuring two-dimensional reflection.
[characters/flatboy.md]

sardine as provisions, on the hypothesis that the mirrors would either reflect
her accurately, which is boring, or show seven simultaneous Scrambles in quantum
superposition, which is vindication.

## With
Flatboy is housed in the mirrors because a flat being is only given depth by
reflection. [record/beings/flatboy.md]
The Professor's hyphae grow in three dimensions through the castle stone, and
where they reach this hall Flatboy sees them only as cross-sections, circles that
appear and disappear, which it finds disturbing.
[characters/flatboy.md]
Bartholomew audits the loop and finds the mandate both met and open; the Echo
Archivist's sound evidence predates the event it describes by four years and is
still not accepted paperwork; Consultant Fluff sleeps through the gift shop,
having never seen her own face either way.
