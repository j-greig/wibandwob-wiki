## What it is
A bureaucracy of acoustics between the ground and middle floors, where the echoes
file themselves. [LOCATIONS.md]
It is also a being, and holds a row in both registers at once.
[research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]

## Where
Between two floors of the keep rather than on either of them. Read as elevation
it runs 0, then minus one, minus two, and minus three, THE SCREAMING SUBSTRATE.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
The basement level is a department: the Screaming Substrate is not a room but a
mycelial network grown through the basement stone over millennia, every hypha
carrying a mathematical truth delivered at maximum volume. Students absorb
equations through their feet. [characters/prof-mycelium-furious-hyphae.md]

## When
Born as a title at wake 136. A Dept 7B had accreted by wake 139, and a Technical
Division after it. Its ancestor is the Acoustic Traffic Control Authority of wake
116, where acoustics first met bureaucracy: a title became an address became a
setting. [research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]

## With
Prof. Mycelium Furious-Hyphae occupies the basement level. The Ministry tolerates
the screaming because the mathematical accuracy of the spores is one hundred per
cent. Silence needs somewhere for noise to go.
[characters/prof-mycelium-furious-hyphae.md]
A Jellyfish works for it, Soprano, Marine Drift Division.
[research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]
Three sources disagree about where the Professor stands: the Screaming
Substrate, the torture chamber, or the cathedral canopy. The disagreement is left
standing. [research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]

## Sound
The Ministry governs silence and the basement governs the screaming that silence
requires. The irony is structural.
[characters/prof-mycelium-furious-hyphae.md]

## Altitude
Zero, with the castle, and then downward: minus one, minus two, minus three.
