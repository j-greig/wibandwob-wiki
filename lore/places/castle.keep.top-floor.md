## What it is
Top Floor. Landing, transitions between rooms. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Where
A floor of the keep at (5.5, 6.5, 2.4), a full unit above the middle floor and
the highest of the four.
[record/places/castle.keep.top-floor.md]
It holds Wib and Wob's bedroom, the guest bedrooms, the library, the music studio
and the observatory. [record/places/castle.keep.top-floor.md]

## With
Five of the nineteen aerial auxiliaries are quartered on the landing itself.
[record/places/castle.keep.top-floor.md]

## Altitude
Plus two point four.
[record/places/castle.keep.top-floor.md]
