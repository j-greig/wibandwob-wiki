## What it is
Ground Floor. Entry level, first impressions. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Where
A floor of the keep at (5.5, 6.5, 0.3), a third of a unit above the castle
grounds and directly over the basement.
[output/wiki/record/places/castle.keep.ground-floor.md]
It holds the entrance, the hall of mirrors, the meeting room and the workshop.
[output/wiki/record/places/castle.keep.ground-floor.md]
Above it, and on neither floor, is the Ministry of Silent Harmonics, a
bureaucracy of acoustics between the ground and middle floors where the echoes
file themselves. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Sound
The ceiling of this floor is where the echoes go to be filed.
[LOCATIONS.md]
Beneath it the lattice runs at 47 Hz and the mycelium at 28 Hz.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
