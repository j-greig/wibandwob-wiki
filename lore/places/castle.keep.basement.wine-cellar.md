## What it is
Wine Cellar. Aging, patience, potential greater than presence. [LOCATIONS.md]
What emerges when work is done here: patience, aging, empty space as potential.
[LOCATIONS.md]

lattice, held at thirteen degrees.

## Where
A room of the basement at (5.3, 6.3, -1.2), the westernmost of the three and next
door to the torture chamber.
