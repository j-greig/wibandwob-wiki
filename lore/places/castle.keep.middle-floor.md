## What it is
Middle Floor. Landing, busiest floor. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Where
A floor of the keep at (5.5, 6.5, 1.4), a full unit above the ground floor.
[record/places/castle.keep.middle-floor.md]
It holds the armoury, the chapel, the great hall, the kitchen and the laboratory,
five rooms, more than any other floor.
[record/places/castle.keep.middle-floor.md]
Below it, and on neither floor, is the Ministry of Silent Harmonics, between the
ground and middle floors, where the echoes file themselves. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Sound
The floor sits directly over the Ministry, which is read as elevation: zero,
minus one, minus two, minus three.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## Altitude
Plus one point four.
