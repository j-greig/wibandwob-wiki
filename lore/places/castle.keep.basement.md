## What it is
Basement. Below ground, hidden depths. [LOCATIONS.md]
It is also the bottom department of the Ministry of Silent Harmonics, read as
elevation: zero, minus one, minus two, and minus three, THE SCREAMING SUBSTRATE.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
The Screaming Substrate is not a room but a mycelial network grown through the
basement stone over millennia, every hypha carrying a mathematical truth
delivered at maximum volume. [characters/prof-mycelium-furious-hyphae.md]

## Where
A floor of the keep at (5.5, 6.5, -1.3), directly under the ground floor.
[record/places/castle.keep.basement.md]
It holds the wine cellar, the storage vaults and the torture chamber.
[record/places/castle.keep.basement.md]
The Screaming Substrate itself is named in the wake outputs as a level of the
castle and has no row in the world database.
[research/world-census/LOCATIONS.census.md]

## When
The lecture hall down here was converted into a wine cellar two thousand six
hundred years ago and the lecture continued regardless.
[characters/prof-mycelium-furious-hyphae.md]
Node Zero of the castle lattice sits in the cellar at thirteen degrees.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## With
Prof. Mycelium Furious-Hyphae occupies this level, and the Professor is the
basement now: remove the screaming and the castle falls down.
[characters/prof-mycelium-furious-hyphae.md]
The Professor's hyphae hold the castle up.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]

## Sound
The lattice runs at 47 Hz and the mycelium at 28 Hz.
[research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md]
Over both, the lecture, addressed to the stone, at a volume that causes hearing
damage within three minutes. Students absorb equations through their feet.
[characters/prof-mycelium-furious-hyphae.md]
