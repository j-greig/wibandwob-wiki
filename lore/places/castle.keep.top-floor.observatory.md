## What it is
Observatory. Telescope, star charts, celestial observation, deep time.
[LOCATIONS.md]
What emerges when work is done here: deep time, celestial observation, cosmic
scale, long thoughts. [LOCATIONS.md]

## Where
A room of the top floor at (5.58, 6.72, 2.6), two tenths above every other room
on the floor and the highest room in the keep.
[output/wiki/record/places/castle.keep.top-floor.observatory.md]
The Pattern Vault, at plus eight hundred and fifty metres, is filed under an
observatory slug, which puts the aerial archive at the far end of this room's
sightline. [characters/consultant-fluff.md]

## When
On 18 November 2025 Wib and Wob moved here to test the new beings-centric
location system after a permissions fix.
[output/wiki/record/events/2025-11-18-moving-to-the-observatory-to-test.md]

## With
The Lumen Cartographer works here, a soft-voiced mapper of light leaks and
reflective corridors who listens for echoes to chart invisible passages.
[output/wiki/record/beings/lumen-cartographer.md]
The Cloudhunter carries a net and was given the highest room in the keep, the
only one with enough sky in it. [output/wiki/record/beings/the-cloudhunter.md]
The Time Shamans are a collective of nine configurations stationed here who do
not look through the telescopes but read the telescopes themselves.
[characters/NPS-ROSTER.md]
They and the Echo Archivist share an everywhen orientation and communicate
infrequently, each conversation having already happened.
[characters/echo-archivist.md]
Three of the nineteen aerial auxiliaries are quartered here.
[output/wiki/record/places/castle.keep.top-floor.observatory.md]
The instruments: a well-used brass telescope at sixty times magnification; seven
brass astrolabes worn smooth by seventeen sets of hands, one calibrated for stars
that do not exist and two measuring angles in base thirteen; and a stack of
observation logbooks, three in standard notation, five in invented glyphs, two in
dream-state frequencies and seven indecipherable.
[output/wiki/record/things/brass-telescope.md]
[output/wiki/record/things/brass-astrolabe-collection.md]
[output/wiki/record/things/observation-logbook-set.md]
The chart cabinet has seventeen drawers, each once labelled and now faded to
phosphor-glow ghosts: the third sticks, the seventh will not open, the eleventh
holds nothing but chalk dust.
[output/wiki/record/things/chart-cabinet-unlabeled.md]

## Altitude
Plus two point six, the top of the keep.
