## What it is
Third of the Seventeen Astronomers, and the one who made the meteor cairn at
Starfall Clearing: a spiral of star-iron, seven layers.
[research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]
On the record, and absent from every roster kept by hand.
[research/world-census/BEINGS.census.md]

## Where
Starfall Clearing, in the western forest, where meteors land more often than
probability allows. [research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md, LOCATIONS.md]

## When
The clearing is where WibWob first met them.
[research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]

## With
The Seventeen Astronomers, of whom two are on the record. They left seventeen
observation logbooks: three standard, five in an invented glyph, two in
dream-frequency, seven indecipherable. Seventeen drawers, seven astrolabes.
[research/world-census/BEINGS.census.md, research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]
The Eleventh Astronomer erased their own archives and left chalk dust in drawer
eleven. [research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md]

## Sound
The cairn is pitched at 528 Hz.
