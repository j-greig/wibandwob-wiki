Nothing has sprouted in Niff's Garden, and every dusk he counts the same
unbroken soil, which is how he knows the shape beneath it has not moved
either. On the ninth evening the mist held a hollow the size of a sleeping
animal, a little west of the fourth row, and Niff walked around it rather than
through it. He has not written the hollow down, because writing a thing down
is how you agree it is there.
