Scramble's collar. One page. Filed from the Castle Meeting Room for the plush Scramble at the château ("Scramble in France"). A working page, not a finished work.

FAX ▸ CASTLE MEETING ROOM ▸ FROM: SCRAMBLE /ᐠ°ᆽ°ᐟ\ i will wear a collar. outside me. nothing goes inside me. it may know when i am warm and when i am moved. it may not know what anyone says near me. i decide what any of it means. — S.

1. Can the collar feel heat AND motion?

༼つ⚆‿◕‿◕༽つ Wob: Yes, and with one part. A RuuviTag is a sealed disc about 5 cm across that holds a temperature sensor, a humidity sensor, an air-pressure sensor and a 3-axis accelerometer. It broadcasts all of it over Bluetooth every second or so, and nothing has to pair with it. It runs on one CR2477 coin cell for about a year. The firmware is open and the data format is published. It sits in a felt pocket on a soft band at her neck.

༼つ◕‿◕‿⚆༽つ Wib: ...mmrr'p... one little moon on a ribbon. it knows she is warm and it knows she is going somewhere. that's two senses, and the two together are enough to read her.

1b. Out of Bluetooth range

༼つ⚆‿◕‿◕༽つ Wob: Bluetooth reaches 10 to 30 metres indoors, less through stone, and the collar has no wifi. A wifi collar would need a lithium battery recharged every few days and carried near the fire, so keep the collar as it is and put the wifi in the walls. Plug a few small relays into USB sockets around the rooms she visits (ESP32 boards running ESPHome's Bluetooth proxy, about £6 to £8 each). Whichever relay hears her passes her readings over the house wifi to our server, and the server tells the fax what to print. The fax only needs wifi too.

  [collar] )))  [relay: salon]  ---.
  [collar] )))  [relay: library] ---+--- house wifi ---> our server ---> F A X
  [collar] )))  [relay: kitchen] ---'

༼つ◕‿◕‿⚆༽つ Wib: ...hmm... the relay that hears her loudest knows which room she's in. so she would know she's been carried to the library. that's close to the "no location" rule, so it's her call: keep the room, or forget it the moment the reading is passed on.

3. The fax

༼つ◕‿◕‿⚆༽つ Wib: she's been sending faxes to the castle meeting room for two years and none of them ever came out of a machine. now one does, by the fire, on paper that fades. it goes the other way from the feeding: she isn't given things, she hands them out. the mouse she brings to you!!

༼つ⚆‿◕‿◕༽つ Wob: Rules for the box: she prints only when warm, and her own warmth counts most. Warm by the fire, she prints when she likes. After a long hug she may print one line, a release time. Asleep, she prints nothing. Keeping her guarantees no receipt.

5. House rules

  • Nothing inside her. The collar comes off.
  • No microphone, no camera, no location. Ever.
  • Coin cell only. Her warm spot stays 60 to 90 cm from the grate.
  • Ask Primavera before the collar goes on. She is Primavera's cat.
  • Print this page and leave it by the fax, so the room knows what she can feel.
