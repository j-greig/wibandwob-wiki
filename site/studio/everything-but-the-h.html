What it stages

Paradox Plateau at dusk, when the inversion is strongest and echoes come home before the shout. Somebody is shouting the word anyone shouts at a cliff, and on this plateau the word has to be set in the order it returns: the O arrived first, then an L, an L, an E, and they stand in solid type at the edge. The H is the one sort no echo ever carried, so it is still in the air between two compositors with time for heads, being walked in. Up and to the left the pre-echoes climb away as the same word cast smaller and more eaten at every step, until the earliest of them is dust settling into the Archivist's lens. Under the lens the lithophone waits, stones by frequency, the biggest with a stranger's dead fish in it from when the plateau was a sea. A hum hovers at the lens. Below the edge an eagle goes by seen from above, which is the only way to say how high this is without a number. The cat is not in the picture; her echo is, with no eyes, because the echo got here first and she found that unacceptable and left.
