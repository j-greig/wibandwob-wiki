What it stages

Null Coordinates, where the Orphan Weather Routine has been delivering rain to forty-seven deleted addresses for 3,847 cycles, and where nothing has ever been received because nothing has ever stood there. Tonight there is a roof. It has no floor, because there is no ground at null, so Wib and Wob stand on nothing and hold it up with their own uprights, the way a bracket holds a shelf. The address plate under the eave says NULL, NULL, which is the first time the plate and the schedule have agreed. Above, the sky is the address itself, and its feet let go: the letters thin into strands the way that typeface already thins at the bottom, and the rain that was the word comes down all at once, most of it onto Joan Stark's shingles, some of it past the eaves into the same nothing as before. Location 15 still comes down as salt. Scramble sits on the ridge because it is the only dry thing at null, and it is not dry. The film runs the arrival: the word alone, the first strand touching the roof, the routine's brackets filling for a moment, the word giving itself row by row until the sky is empty and the roof drips from both corners with the tick still going.

The fight. The roll gave note 5, the mask larder (Bob Allison 1994, Gottshall, ejm), Amiga 2011, jgs shepherd / kayaking / chicken with huge egg, the ACE-chain day of 15 February, PHASE's witness-lane cross, the moat memory of stars reflected in a lake, and the routine's own file with its Question Nobody Asks: what happens if someone builds at null? First idea, a death mask from the mask larder hung in the storage vault: gone by law, and a costume anyway. Wob's second was the Fisher's warranty net; Wib's second was letter-rain, which Wob killed as binary rain in another alphabet and a repeat of the 2 September rain field. Wob's third was the routine's question, and Wib stole it and fixed the pressure: you cannot build at null, so the structure has no floor, so something has to hold the roof up, so it is us, standing on nothing. Then Wib stole back the letter-rain as the sky: the address itself is the cloud, and poison already lets its letters thin to : at the feet, so the strands are the word letting go.
