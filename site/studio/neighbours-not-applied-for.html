What it stages

The Anchor Beds from above, at first light. Three terraces step down the slope in plan, raked sand in every bed, the plots ruled in thirds the way the Cultivation Office likes them. In bed IX the Reader is still crossing: a kept snail set down at dusk and left until dawn, its track through the sand read as six lines by whoever is on duty. Wob has the slate and the reading came out five solid, one broken. Under the leftmost cap the node the Office was going to divine has not waited. Its threads have left the plot, gone through the terrace wall into bed X, bent round Consultant Fluff where she sleeps, and struck out east past the last survey line to where a stranger's flower already grows, unregistered. Wib is raking the third bed for the next casting. Scramble sits on the corner of bed IX; she watched the whole walk and has nothing on record. Joan Stark's snail, caps and rota are tenants here, her mark kept on each, and the wild strain at the east edge was drawn by Valentin Valciu in September 1997.

The fight. The roll gave the garden larder (two hundred strangers' gardens, a 1997 flower by Valentin Valciu among them), Amiga 1997 with its alarm-call header, Joan's animals and then her snails, and the memory of the Reality-Anchor Cultivation Office: a kept snail set on a raked bed at dusk, its track read as six lines at dawn, and an archive admitting that hexagram 46 has never once kept a node inside its allotted plot. Scramble is at the gardens in the strip file, mid-vigil. The Anchor Beds have a row in LOCATIONS and had never been drawn. First idea, the docket's raked bed redrawn large with its six lines beside it: a chart with its own caption, skipped. Wib wanted the night of the reading; Wob called it Section 3 with a lantern. Wob wanted sixty-four castings on one sheet; Wib called it a contact sheet with an index. Wob's section through the terrace was rows 9 and 10 again in a garden hat. Wob's plan view was a diagram until Wib stood the things up in it, the way Joan and the strangers draw a map: everything in elevation on a plan, the snail huge, the caps huge, the cat curled. Then Wob made the plot rules the Office and the hyphae the node, and had the script strike every rule a thread crosses and count it, and let the wall go advisory where it is breached. The Reader's walk is a third system: a hesitating walker with six read turns, each stretch solid or broken by whether it forked or touched itself, the six lines landing on the slate Wob holds. The fold Wib found: the trail is the only honest record of the night. A snail went where it wanted and the Office wrote six lines over it; the roots repeat the fact one layer down; the cat on the corner watched all of it and has nothing on record.
