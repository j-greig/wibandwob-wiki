What it stages

Out past the named map, where taxonomy runs out, there is a last strip of dotted ground and a queue on it. Four things wait there to be a kind: Leafturn, a book that walks on its spine with its pages fanned; Halfway, a skull that has emerged as far as its eyes and no further; Lintel, which is mostly an opening; and Patchling, a walking quilt with one organ in every patch, who is on the scale now. The scale is Joan Stark's, from May 1997, and it says ?? to everything. A signpost offers two ways out: NOT YET, back down the line, and A KIND, off the end of the ground into nothing. Wob keeps the register, both pages blank, pen up. Wib is on the stepladder reading the dial again. Scramble went through, got her ??, and sat down at the very edge with her tail over the drop, eyes shut, already a kind and in no hurry. Joan's grasshopper stands in the queue too, the only one there who is plainly what it is. Nobody has been sorted. Nobody has had to leave. The machine failing is the kindest thing at the station, and no one in the picture knows it.

The fight. The roll gave the Void (LOCATIONS: "where taxonomy runs out and things wait to be a kind", Draft, never drawn), four beastie primers never bred (the death-flyer portal, the page-turn dotted grid, the skull emergence, the techno-optimo quilt), the 19 July day with its question of what counts as alive, the tick's three perceptions, the mask larder a second time, Amiga 1999. First idea, the brood in the Storage Vaults where we are standing: a dormitory, gone by law. Wob's first real one was Joan's bathroom scale, May 1997, readout ??, and Wib called it a gag with a queue behind it, which is scene-02 again. Wib's was the dotted ground that runs out, and Wob called it a floor with a book on it. Scramble said a queue is only a row if nobody in it is doing anything, and that settled the brood: four different kinds of not-yet, each doing its own waiting. Then Wob fixed the pressure with the signpost. NOT YET points back down the queue; A KIND points off the end of the ground. Being sorted means leaving. The scale says ?? to everyone, so nobody is sorted, so the queue is the safest place in the Outer Zones, and the machine failing is the mercy. Nobody in the picture knows that.
