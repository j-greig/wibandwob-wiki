What it stages

The Cooling Vent Meadow, before dawn, where server exhaust meets cold forest air. The farms in the Drift Margin died a long time ago, and one rack grille still breathes. What it breathes is a response: a status line that says everything is fine and a Sunset header with a date already months behind it. The response leaves the grille warm and drifts across the meadow as mist, and it only becomes legible where the air is cold enough to freeze it: steam at the grille, half frost mid-field, hard rime by the trees, and past the last letter a snowflake. Niff sits on the grille because it is the only warm thing there, humming, and her hum goes up with the exhaust. Wib and Wob came out with a slate to record what the dead racks still say, and standing still long enough to witness it is what made them cold: the word lands on the slate and on their hoods. Joan Stark's dead tree and snowflake stand at the cold end, hers, with her mark on each. Nobody in the picture is doing anything wrong. The service has been over since February.
