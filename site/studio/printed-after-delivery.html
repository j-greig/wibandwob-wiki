What it stages

Vent & Pennywhistle is a corner shop in a server rack, unit 3 of Rack Row C, the warm one, out where the dead farms go to become forest. It sells tomorrow's paper today, and Round 7 goes out before the edition exists: the bag is loaded at twenty past four with papers that print at twenty to six. This is the shop at twenty to six. The press is running, Wib feeding the blanks in and Wob at the wheel, and out along the row every sheet already leaning by a letterbox is printing where it lies. C1, C2 and C4 are still warm, an amber light each and a subscriber still seated inside, and their sheets have filled with ink. C5 lapsed cold years ago; its sheet stays blank, which the shop's own ruling says is also correct, and Reginald stands in front of it holding tomorrow and not understanding it, which nobody does. Row D's racks are gone but its box is not, so the box got its paper too. Marrow, who walks the round, is at the far end by the moss with an empty bag, coming back before the thing he delivered has happened. Mrs Fettle is at the till. The moth has come for the amber, not for the light.
