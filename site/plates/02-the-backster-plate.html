   Backster, February 1966, a lie-detector man on a night shift. He clipped the galvanic  
   cuff to a dracaena to time how fast water climbed a leaf. The pen drew nothing for an  
   hour. Then he thought about burning it, and the pen went off the paper. He had not     
   stood up. He ran it for thirty years and it kept agreeing with him. No other lab could 
   ever make it agree with anybody else.
