clustercrabs-v3b

A top-down floor plan of a concert stage: a grand-piano outline seen from above with its harp of strings, overlaid by a tessellated grid of identical small clockwork crabs in marching formation, each tagged with a direction arrow, ringed by a coordinate ruler and a tracker legend. The swarm reads as one cellular field crossing the instrument, kasakasa, a minimap of vermin playing by occupation.
