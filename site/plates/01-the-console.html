01-the-console

A tall monochrome instrument panel: an embossed P(doom) bezel over a DOOM-style status bar where a grimacing dotted face-gauge sits between CONFIDENCE, TIMELINE and CONTROL readouts, a creeping reactor probability bar, mitigation weapon-slots and a binary telemetry grid. The needle does not flinch; the dread is in the precision, the slow climb of a number nobody watching can lower.
