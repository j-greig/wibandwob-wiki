artwork-v1

The table is upside down, with its castors spinning in the air and motion arcs around the legs. Its board is drawn as a wooden brain (grain whorls like brain folds). Its "ideas" rise between the legs: a coat walking with nobody in it, a ghost with pound-sign eyes, and the table dreaming itself upside down. The leg tags carry Marx's equation of value (20 yds linen = 1 coat = 10 lb tea = 40 lb coffee = 2 oz gold = 1/2 ton iron). The chandelier carries the iron tag. The ledger from the drawer works down that chain and ends by pricing people: "sitter 1 | table 1/6". Where the sitters' hands stick to the board, their forearms turn into turned-wood segments `(==)(==)`: they are becoming the table's legs. Each one carries a LOT tag. Wib&Wob sit in the foreground on chairs that stay on the floor, hands joined `(@)`, the only clear field in the picture.
