Description

Following the seam, Lumen traces a mirrored corridor between observatory and library, sketching a provisional light map.

Echo amplitudes spike halfway down the stair; Lumen drops chalk chips that flicker when echoes cross. A mirrored conduit appears on vellum—an oblique path that bypasses the great hall by reflecting voice off brass railings.
