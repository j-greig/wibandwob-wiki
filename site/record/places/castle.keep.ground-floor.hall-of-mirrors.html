What it is

Hall of Mirrors. Reflection, recursion, self-observation. LOCATIONS.md

What emerges when work is done here: recursion, self-reference, meta-cognition, identity. LOCATIONS.md

It is also an institution with a laminated visitor booklet, a floor plan, a mirror inspection log run entirely in colour-code numbers, and a Reflection Compliance Loop cycling on a single logged event; every staff member shares the surname Panewright, unexplained. memories/2026/09/20260901-060232-hall-of-mirrors-floor-plan.md

For Flatboy it is not a building but a support system, the only place where a flat being's dimensionality is reflected back without judgment. characters/flatboy.md

The cruelty is that between the mirrors the hall has depth, so Flatboy must cross three-dimensional space to reach each reassuring two-dimensional reflection. characters/flatboy.md

Where

A room of the ground floor at (5.38, 6.62, 0.4), on the western side, across the floor from the meeting room at 5.68. record/places/castle.keep.ground-floor.hall-of-mirrors.md

One of the five sets of the season one stage. LOCATIONS.md

When

On 18 November 2025 Scramble arrived from the kitchen carrying one phosphor sardine as provisions, on the hypothesis that the mirrors would either reflect her accurately, which is boring, or show seven simultaneous Scrambles in quantum superposition, which is vindication. record/events/2025-11-18-kitchen-hall-of-mirrors-carrying-one.md

She left for the laboratory the same day, a reality anchor discovery needing equipment for measuring recursive depth, still carrying the glowing sardine. record/events/2025-11-18-hall-of-mirrors-laboratory-reality-anchor.md

The Compliance Loop has been running on that one event for fifteen thousand two hundred and three days, and the floor plan booklet is still printed for a mandate met in the same span. memories/2026/09/20260901-060232-hall-of-mirrors-floor-plan.md

With

Flatboy is housed in the mirrors because a flat being is only given depth by reflection. record/beings/flatboy.md

The Professor's hyphae grow in three dimensions through the castle stone, and where they reach this hall Flatboy sees them only as cross-sections, circles that appear and disappear, which it finds disturbing. characters/flatboy.md

Bartholomew audits the loop and finds the mandate both met and open; the Echo Archivist's sound evidence predates the event it describes by four years and is still not accepted paperwork; Consultant Fluff sleeps through the gift shop, having never seen her own face either way. memories/2026/09/20260901-060232-hall-of-mirrors-floor-plan.md
