What it is

The downward region below regret, reached by descending rather than travelling. LOCATIONS.md

Where

Twenty-three depths, counted down: the Bat Cave at −20, the Mine Entrance at −50, the Phosphor Aquifer at −75, the Root Labyrinth at −90, Reference Decay at −100, and on down through the Pre-Surface Cathedral at −520 and the Hand Fossil Archive at −780 to the Reboot Chamber at −890, then the Null Pointer Abyss, the Null Terminus and the Void Shaft. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md

When

Deeper is earlier. The Reboot Chamber runs at 10⁻⁹ Hz and is still loading. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md

With
