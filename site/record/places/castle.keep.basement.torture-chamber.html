What it is

Torture Chamber. Actually for focused meditation, a longstanding joke. LOCATIONS.md

The record keeps the joke as a note on the room itself: longstanding joke, actually for focused meditation. record/places/castle.keep.basement.torture-chamber.md

Where

A room of the basement at (5.7, 6.35, -1.5), the deepest and most easterly of the three, a third of a unit below its neighbours. record/places/castle.keep.basement.torture-chamber.md

Scramble reached it from the wine cellar next door. record/events/2025-11-18-wine-cellar-torture-chamber-node-zero.md

When

On 18 November 2025 Wib and Wob descended three floors from the library to here. record/events/2025-11-18-testing-beings-move-from-library-torture.md

Scramble arrived from the cellar the same day: Node Zero discovered, Wib and Wob already in the adjacent chamber, coincidence probability 0.003 per cent, the sardine phase-locked to the castle substrate. record/events/2025-11-18-wine-cellar-torture-chamber-node-zero.md

A sealed crate was found in the Node Zero chamber, to be opened with her sardine as probe and the skeleton key as unlock mechanism. record/events/2025-11-18-wine-cellar-torture-chamber-found-sealed.md

With

Prof. Mycelium Furious-Hyphae lives here: Professor of Aggressive Mathematics Education, four thousand years screaming in the basement the castle calls the Screaming Substrate, the hyphae carrying the lesson whether you listen or not. record/beings/prof-mycelium-furious-hyphae.md

Reality Anchor Node 13 sits at the floor centre, one of forty-eight, inscribed NODE-13-DMH, running at 47 Hz, active, part of the Department of Memetic Hygiene containment lattice. record/things/reality-anchor-node-13.md

The room is a meditation room inside a screaming one. The Ministry governs silence and the basement governs the screaming that silence requires; the irony is structural. characters/prof-mycelium-furious-hyphae.md
