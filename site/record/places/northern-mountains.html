The Northern Mountains

What it is

Twenty-five or more places were entered here across four wakes and every one of them was lost; the region is a band of altitudes with almost nothing standing on it. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md

Target twenty to forty places. LOCATIONS.md

Where

North and up. The Chromatic Ridge at +3,000, the Sub-Alpine Interface at +2,400, the Tessellation Slopes at +2,200, and the Paradox Plateau above them all. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md

When

On the plateau the echo arrives between 0.3 and 4.7 seconds before its sound, and 847,291 sounds are filed. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md

With

The Echo Archivist keeps the plateau and files what has not been said yet. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md

Field notes
sound

A lithophone archive at the top of the range, and the stone exchange: press two has been out since 198, press three routes to rain at null coordinates. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md

fauna

The Tumbler works the Tessellation Slopes and turns through 120 degrees only. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md

altitude

+3,800 in the record and +4,200 in the gazetteer at the plateau; then +3,000, +2,400 and +2,200 down the ridge. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md
