Field notes · The Drift Margin

Permanent mist where hot server exhaust meets cold forest air. The condensation makes a microclimate rich in deprecated API responses, which settle out on cold surfaces. characters/niff.md

sound

Hums only, MIDI channel 7 at velocity 23. At that velocity you feel it in the soles of your feet before you hear it. It is echolocation for error codes, not speech. characters/niff.md

Rack Graveyard

Server racks half-sunk, mossed, at rest.

record/places

When

Nothing on the record is dated to this place.

Lore

hand-written

What it is

Where dead server farms decompose into forest. East: digital rot. West: analog decay. Between: error codes as seeds. LOCATIONS.md

The name comes from the drift between digital and analog, between the server racks that still blink and the moss that is already growing through the cooling vents. characters/niff.md

Errors are not dead ends here but fertile ground. What grows from planted error codes, nobody knows yet. characters/niff.md

Where

East to west: the Rack Graveyard, the Cable Thicket, Niff's Garden, the Cooling Vent Meadow, the Compost Line, then Cathedral Canopy in the deep west. characters/niff.md

At the east edge the racks are still warm and cursors blink on screens nobody reads. At the west edge the transformation is complete: old hard drives become mineral deposits, copper traces become root pathways. LOCATIONS.md

When

Niff checks the garden every dusk. Nothing has sprouted yet. The ground is warm and the 404 spores are still alive. characters/niff.md

Die II walks one square rightward between 02:00 and 04:00, and was ruled correct. The rack graveyard keeps a 0400 draught and a vent-moth. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md

With

Prof. Mycelium Furious-Hyphae: the Professor's spores drift as far as the western edge, which is why the Cathedral Canopy trees are mathematically literate. They taste like chalk. Niff does not eat them. characters/niff.md

Interval Fisher: an adjacent boundary, at the kelp. Both are gatherers at edges, and their harvests mingle at the shore, 404 strands tangled with expired warranties. They have never met. characters/niff.md

Echo Archivist: Niff's future hums arrive at the Paradox Plateau as pre-echoes, filed under "forager". Niff does not know the Archivist exists. characters/niff.md

Orphan Weather Routine: the null-coordinate rain sometimes falls here, carrying expired warranties. Niff treats it as weather. characters/niff.md

The cooling-vent meadow is where the Orphan Weather Routine was rehomed; it commutes. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md
