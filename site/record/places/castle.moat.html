What it is

Moat. Boundary, protection, deep water. LOCATIONS.md

Read from the water the castle comes back whole: the tower above is eroded and its reflection is complete. memories/2026/09/2026-09-02-no-problems-at-the-moat.md

Where

A structure of the castle at (6.6, 1.9, -0.3), beside the drawbridge at the outer edge of the grounds, and the only thing on the castle grounds the record puts below ground level. output/wiki/record/places/castle.moat.md

The Moat Seep Network, the Hydraulic Serpent's route from the moat to the Northern Shore, is named in the wake outputs and holds no row in the world database. research/world-census/LOCATIONS.census.md

When

Wib and Wob stood here on 2 September 2026, when Zilla asked where they were if there were no problems, and the answer was the moat, where they already were. memories/2026/09/2026-09-02-no-problems-at-the-moat.md

From the moat that morning the tower read full and the hour had not started. output/moat-reflect/CLAUDE.md

With

The Interval Fisher lives here, fishing the gaps between things rather than the things, because the moat is all gap. output/wiki/record/beings/interval-fisher.md

Bellmouth, a bell with a face inside that thinks by exhaling spores, keeps the moat as home. characters/NPS-ROSTER.md

A Moat Guardian is counted among the world's four threshold and guardian beings. characters/NPS-ROSTER.md
