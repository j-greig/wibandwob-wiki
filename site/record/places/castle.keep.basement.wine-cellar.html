What it is

Wine Cellar. Aging, patience, potential greater than presence. LOCATIONS.md

What emerges when work is done here: patience, aging, empty space as potential. LOCATIONS.md

It is also Node Zero's chamber, the origin of the castle's forty-eight node lattice, held at thirteen degrees. research/world-census/LORE-DIGEST-2026-09-02.md

Where

A room of the basement at (5.3, 6.3, -1.2), the westernmost of the three and next door to the torture chamber. record/places/castle.keep.basement.wine-cellar.md

When

It was a lecture hall until two thousand six hundred years ago, when it was converted; the lecture continued regardless. characters/prof-mycelium-furious-hyphae.md

On 18 November 2025 Scramble came down from the laboratory following an infrastructure thread of Node 47, the 47 Hz resonance and Wob's recalibration notes, her sardine oscillating in sympathy with the castle frequency. record/events/2025-11-18-laboratory-wine-cellar-following-infrastructure-thread.md
