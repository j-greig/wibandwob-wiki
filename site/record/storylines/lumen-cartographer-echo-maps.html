Lumen Cartographer charts the starlight echo lattice in the observatory dome, tagging nodes where stone hums back.
Standing beneath the rotating dome, Lumen holds prisms to catch faint leaks. They whistle a low tone and listen for the castle’s reply, marking every resonance on vellum. A dull harmonic reveals a hidden seam toward the library.

Following the seam, Lumen traces a mirrored corridor between observatory and library, sketching a provisional light map.
