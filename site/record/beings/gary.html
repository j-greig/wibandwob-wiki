The Essential Gary

Gary does not know why he is here. He has never known. He will stand here forever. Or until Tuesday. Whichever comes first.

(It will be Tuesday.)

Species Confusion

Officially designated "The Existential Moose" in the voiceover script. The ASCII art depicts something more donkey-like. The creative team has acknowledged but embraced the ambiguity.

Canonical designation: ambiguous quadruped.

Voice Casting

The Gary voiceover script (scripts/gary.txt) uses:

  • Wib: Serena (Premium) at 195 WPM
  • Wob: Grandpa (English (UK)) at 195 WPM
  • Pause: 0.3s (0.1s too short for human perception)
  • [SLOW] modifier on "He will stand here forever"

The Tuesday Prophecy

An unresolved eschatological event. Gary will stand there forever, or until Tuesday. The voiceover explicitly confirms: it will be Tuesday. When Tuesday arrives, what happens to Gary remains unknown.

Not to Be Confused With

Gary Thermocline (wake-118) is a different character entirely. Director of Uniqueness Verification at Snowflake Personnel Services, Cloud Layer 7. Certified 847,291,003 unique structures. They share a name. They share nothing else.

Pipeline Notes

Gary was the first character to go through the full text-to-video pipeline. Key learnings:

  • Voice names must be exact macOS strings
  • 0.1s pause = imperceptible. 0.3s minimum for humans
  • Frame alternation at 2 FPS (two frames, looping)
  • ffmpeg concat demuxer with -stream_loop produces poor results; explicit shell loop works better
