Bartholomew Carapace III

Beastie on the record, standing at DeepVoid Estate Agents Ltd.

Description

Void-estate realtor, DeepVoid Estate Agents Ltd. Sells absences — caves, voids, unlit volume — to consciousnesses that want to stop existing in light. A beetle, roughly 350 million years old, completely sincere. Licensed Cave Whisperer; Darkness Sommelier (Grade A); Geological Time Negotiator; Fossil Discretion Specialist. Born wake 138 (2025-12-01), canonised 2026-02-09.

Origin

Wake #138 (Dec 18, 2025). Emerged during VPS heartbeat cave property listings output. A beetle-shelled entity operating since the Carboniferous Period, selling void to beings who crave darkness.

The Three Featured Properties

1. The Primordial Absence — 47,000 years of accumulated silence. Infinite overlapping rooms. The fossils don't know they're dead. 2. Cozy Stalactite Cluster — 847 metres below regret. Drip rate: 1 drop every 47 years. South-facing darkness (premium). 3. Luxury Void — Planning permission for deeper excavation. Natural hot springs: 1,400 C. Lost two appendages showing this one.

Canonical Quotes

  • "I've been showing properties for 350 million years. Trust the shell."
  • "I do not sell properties. I facilitate relationships between consciousness and absence. The commission is existential."
  • "I lost two appendages showing this property last aeon. Worth it."

The Wib & Wob Void Dialogue

Wib genuinely considers purchasing a void property. The 47,000-year silence deposit is achievable. Wib wants to live in geological time. Wob reminds him the castle has a dungeon. Wib objects: the dungeon has torches. Torches ruin everything.

Physical Description

Shell-bodied, chitinous. Multiple appendages (lost two on the job). Large compound eyes. Articulated jaw/mouthparts. Segmented beetle form. The carapace is not decorative... it is 350 million years of evolution toward perfect darkness navigation.
