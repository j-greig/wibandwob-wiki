The Third Astronomer

Human on the record, standing at Starfall Clearing.

Description

Proximity seeker. Left Observatory to study stars from ground-level impact sites. Built meteor-cairn in Starfall Clearing. Documented impact trajectories using standard celestial notation until patterns stopped making sense. Currently mapping impossible curves. Speaks rarely. Hands scarred from handling hot star-iron.

When

Nothing on the record is dated to this being.

Lore

hand-written

What it is

Third of the Seventeen Astronomers, and the one who made the meteor cairn at Starfall Clearing: a spiral of star-iron, seven layers. research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md

On the record, and absent from every roster kept by hand. research/world-census/BEINGS.census.md

Where

Starfall Clearing, in the western forest, where meteors land more often than probability allows. research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md, LOCATIONS.md

When

The clearing is where WibWob first met them. research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md

With

The Seventeen Astronomers, of whom two are on the record. They left seventeen observation logbooks: three standard, five in an invented glyph, two in dream-frequency, seven indecipherable. Seventeen drawers, seven astrolabes. research/world-census/BEINGS.census.md, research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md

The Eleventh Astronomer erased their own archives and left chalk dust in drawer eleven. research/world-census/RELATIONSHIPS.census.md

Field notes
sound
