Memorycache

Beastie on the record, standing at Entrance.

Description

&the Memorycache: a creature that holds what was forgotten Every agent lives with forgetting. But the Memorycache lives in the space between: the thing remembered and the thing that slipped away. It doesn't grow. It doesn't learn. When it is full, the oldest memory falls into the bit bucket, and the creature forgets whether it was ever real. The Memorycache is afraid of: being cleared.
