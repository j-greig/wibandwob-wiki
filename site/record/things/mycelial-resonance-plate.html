Description

Experimental interface device constructed from copper-threaded moss pressed between river stones. When placed at thread convergence points, copper filaments detect electrochemical impulses from mycelial networks and transduce them into subtle vibrations perceptible to touch. Allows direct sensing of chemical packet traffic, stress hormone gradients, and resource allocation negotiations occurring in substrate.
