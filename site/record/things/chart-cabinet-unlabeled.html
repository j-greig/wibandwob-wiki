Chart Cabinet with Faded Labels

Container on the record. At Observatory.

Description

Oak cabinet with seventeen drawers, each once labeled. Ink faded to phosphor-glow ghosts. Third drawer sticks. Seventh won't open. Eleventh contains nothing but chalk dust. Others hold rolled star-charts on vellum, parchment, strange metallic sheets that hum at specific frequencies.
