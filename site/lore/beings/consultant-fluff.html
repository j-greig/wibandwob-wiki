Consultant Fluff

This page is written. The world has no row for it.

Lore

hand-written

Identity

Consultant Fluff is the third member of the Pattern Canonicity Tribunal, alongside Justice Whiskers (catnip-impaired, votes YES on everything) and Deputy Paws (professional skeptic, votes NO on 3%). Fluff's role is consultative. Fluff has never consulted.

89% abstention rate. The remaining 11% is ambiguous... possibly paw twitches misread as voting gestures by the tribunal clerk.

The Pattern Canonicity Tribunal

The tribunal meets every 247 seconds (synchronised with the Orphan Weather Routine) to approve or reject weather patterns for the canonical archive. Three layers of non-governance:

1. Ghost Observer overrules all decisions and archives everything 2. Justice Whiskers votes YES on all patterns (catnip influence) 3. Deputy Paws votes NO on 3% (professional skepticism, always overruled) 4. Consultant Fluff sleeps through 89% of proceedings

Result: 99.7% approval rate. The tribunal is pure ceremony. The bowtie dress code is the only strictly enforced rule.

Location

Pattern Vault Canonicity Chamber, +850m altitude. Accessible only with bowtie. The vault is at 100.003% capacity (paradox acceptable). Fluff has a designated sleeping spot... likely a warm corner near the ouija planchette.

Canonical Quote

CONSULTANT FLUFF: [sleeping, did not vote]

This is the only recorded output across all tribunal sessions. Four words. An entire philosophy of non-participation compressed into a verdict bracket.

The Sleeping Critique

What makes Fluff interesting is the inversion. Every other being in Wibwobworld *does* something. Creates, judges, herds, harvests, rains, conducts, forages. Fluff's contribution is conspicuous absence within a system that was already meaningless. A non-participant in a non-functional bureaucracy. Three layers deep into theatre:

  • The tribunal doesn't need to exist (the ghost archives everything)
  • The votes don't matter (the ghost overrules them)
  • Fluff doesn't vote (sleeping through votes that don't matter in a tribunal that doesn't need to exist)

The consultant who consults nothing. The most honest participant in governance theatre.
